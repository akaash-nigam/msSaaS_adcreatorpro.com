## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. **Visit AdCreatorPro**: Navigate to the homepage
2. **Click "Sign Up"**: Located in the top-right corner
3. **Fill in your details**:
   - Email address
   - Password (minimum 6 characters)
   - Display name
4. **Verify your email**: Check your inbox for a verification email
5. **Click the verification link**: This activates your account

**Alternative:** You can also sign up using your Google account for faster registration.

### First Login

After creating your account:
- You'll be redirected to your **Dashboard**
- You'll start with the **Free tier** (3 ads)
- You can begin generating ads immediately

## Creating Your First Ad

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Navigate to the homepage** (click the logo or "Home" link)

2. **Describe your product or service**:
   - Enter a clear, detailed description
   - Example: "Premium organic coffee beans sourced from Colombian farms"
   - Be specific about features and benefits

3. **Select your platform**:
   - Facebook/Instagram
   - LinkedIn
   - Google Ads
   - Twitter
   - TikTok
   - Pinterest

   Each platform generates ads in the appropriate format.

4. **Choose your tone** (optional):
   - Professional
   - Casual
   - Playful
   - Urgent
   - Inspirational
   - Leave blank for AI to decide

5. **Define your target audience** (optional):
   - Example: "Coffee enthusiasts aged 25-45"
   - Example: "Small business owners"
   - Example: "Health-conscious millennials"

6. **Select number of variations** (1-5):
   - Use the slider to choose how many different versions you want
   - Each variation counts as one ad credit
   - Variations help with A/B testing

7. **Click "Generate Ad"**:
   - Wait 3-10 seconds for AI to create your ads
   - Ads will appear below the form

### Understanding the Results

Your generated ads will include:

**For Facebook/Instagram:**
- **Headline**: Attention-grabbing title (max 40 characters)
- **Copy**: Main ad text (max 125 characters)
- **Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Action button text
- **Hashtags**: Relevant hashtags for discovery

**For LinkedIn:**
- **Title**: Professional headline (max 70 characters)
- **Hook**: Opening line to grab attention
- **Body**: Main content (max 600 characters)
- **CTA**: Professional call-to-action

**For Google Ads:**
- **Headline**: Search ad headline (max 30 characters)
- **Description**: Ad description (max 90 characters)
- **CTA**: Action phrase

### Copying Your Ads

**Copy individual fields:**
- Click the copy icon next to any field
- A toast notification confirms the copy

**Copy entire ad:**
- Click "Copy" button below the ad
- All fields are formatted and copied to clipboard

**Copy all variations:**
- Click "Copy All Variations" button
- All variations are formatted as a numbered list
- Perfect for saving to a document or spreadsheet

## Understanding Ad Variations

### What are variations?

Variations are different versions of ads for the same product, generated in a single request.

### Why use variations?

1. **A/B Testing**: Test which version performs better
2. **Different angles**: Each variation emphasizes different features
3. **Audience segments**: Different messages for different audiences
4. **Platform testing**: See which style works best

### How to use variations effectively:

1. **Generate 3-5 variations** for important campaigns
2. **Review all options**: Pick your favorites
3. **Test performance**: Run multiple versions simultaneously
4. **Track results**: See which ads get better engagement
5. **Iterate**: Generate more variations based on what works

### Example Variations

For "Premium coffee beans":

**Variation 1** (Feature-focused):
- Headline: "100% Colombian Coffee Beans"
- Copy: "Experience authentic Colombian coffee. Premium, organic, freshly roasted."

**Variation 2** (Benefit-focused):
- Headline: "Wake Up to Better Coffee"
- Copy: "Transform your morning routine with our artisanal beans. Rich flavor, perfect aroma."

**Variation 3** (Social proof):
- Headline: "5,000+ Coffee Lovers Can't Be Wrong"
- Copy: "Join thousands who've upgraded their coffee game with our premium beans."

## Using Brand Profiles

Brand profiles help you generate consistent ads by saving your brand information.

### Creating a Brand Profile

1. **Navigate to Brand Profiles**: Click in the navigation menu
2. **Click "Create New Profile"**
3. **Fill in your brand details**:

   **Brand Name** (required):
   - Your company or product name
   - Example: "Brew Masters Coffee"

   **Industry** (optional):
   - Your business category
   - Example: "Food & Beverage"

   **Brand Voice** (optional):
   - How your brand communicates
   - Example: "Warm, friendly, and knowledgeable"

   **Target Audience** (optional):
   - Who you're marketing to
   - Example: "Coffee enthusiasts, ages 25-50, urban professionals"

   **Unique Selling Points** (optional):
   - What makes you different
   - Example: "Organic, fair-trade, small-batch roasted"

4. **Set as Default** (optional):
   - Check this box to auto-fill forms with this profile
   - You can only have one default profile

5. **Click "Save"**

### Using a Brand Profile

**With a default profile:**
- The ad generation form auto-fills with your brand information
- You can still edit any field before generating

**Without a default profile:**
- Select a profile from the dropdown
- Form fills with that profile's information

**Benefits:**
- Consistent brand messaging
- Faster ad generation
- Better AI results (more context)
- No need to re-enter information

### Managing Brand Profiles

**Edit a profile:**
1. Go to Brand Profiles
2. Click "Edit" on the profile
3. Update information
4. Click "Save"

**Delete a profile:**
1. Click "Delete" on the profile
2. Confirm deletion
3. Associated ads remain in your history

**Change default profile:**
1. Edit any profile
2. Check "Set as Default"
3. Previous default is automatically unset

### Best Practices

- Create separate profiles for different products/services
- Update profiles as your brand evolves
- Use detailed USPs for better ad generation
- Keep brand voice consistent across profiles

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Facebook/Instagram Ads

**Character Limits:**
- Headline: 40 characters
- Primary text: 125 characters (link ads)
- CTA: Pre-defined buttons

**Best Practices:**
- Use emojis for visual appeal
- Keep it conversational and engaging
- Include a clear call-to-action
- Use hashtags (3-5 relevant ones)
- Test different headline styles

**Example:**
```
Headline: ☕ Premium Coffee Delivered
Copy: Wake up to freshly roasted beans at your door.
      Organic, fair-trade, delicious. Free shipping!
CTA: Shop Now
Hashtags: #coffee #organic #coffeelover
```

### LinkedIn Ads

**Character Limits:**
- Headline: 70 characters
- Intro text: 150 characters
- Body: 600 characters

**Best Practices:**
- Professional, value-focused tone
- Highlight ROI and business benefits
- Use statistics and data
- Address pain points
- B2B language

**Example:**
```
Title: Boost Your Team's Productivity by 30%
Hook: Is your team spending too much time on repetitive tasks?
Body: Our AI-powered platform automates workflows,
      saving 10+ hours per week per employee...
CTA: Request Demo
```

### Google Ads

**Character Limits:**
- Headlines: 30 characters (up to 3)
- Descriptions: 90 characters (up to 2)

**Best Practices:**
- Include target keywords
- Clear value proposition
- Use action words
- Match search intent
- Test different headlines

**Example:**
```
Headline 1: Premium Coffee Beans
Headline 2: Organic & Fair Trade
Headline 3: Free Shipping Today
Description: Fresh roasted Colombian coffee.
            Order now and save 20% on your first bag.
```

### Twitter Ads

**Character Limit:**
- 280 characters total

**Best Practices:**
- Concise and punchy
- Use trending hashtags
- Include visuals (if image ad)
- Clear CTA
- Conversational tone

### TikTok Ads

**Best Practices:**
- Authentic, not overly polished
- Hook viewers in first 2 seconds
- Trending sounds and formats
- User-generated content style
- Fun and entertaining

### Pinterest Ads

**Best Practices:**
- Vertical format (2:3 ratio)
- Descriptive, keyword-rich text
- Inspirational tone
- Step-by-step or how-to content
- Lifestyle imagery

**Access subscription management:**
1. Go to Dashboard
2. Click "Manage Subscription"
3. Opens Stripe Customer Portal

**In the portal you can:**
- Update payment method
- View invoices
- Upgrade/downgrade plan
- Cancel subscription
- Update billing information

**Upgrading:**
- Immediate access to new tier
- Prorated billing

**Downgrading:**
- Takes effect at next billing cycle
- Keep current tier until then

**Canceling:**
- Keeps access until end of billing period
- Reverts to Free tier after

### Billing Cycle

- **Subscriptions**: Billed monthly on signup date
- **Renewals**: Automatic on billing date
- **Ad refresh**: Monthly ad count resets on billing date
- **Failed payments**: 3 retry attempts, then downgrade to Free

### Invoices

- Sent via email after each payment
- Available in Stripe Customer Portal
- Includes billing details and amount

## Tips for Better AI-Generated Ads

### 1. Be Specific in Product Description

**Bad:**
```
Product: Coffee
```

**Good:**
```
Product: Premium organic Colombian coffee beans,
         medium roast, chocolatey notes with hints
         of caramel, ethically sourced
```

### 2. Provide Context

Include:
- Key features
- Main benefits
- Target use case
- Price point (if relevant)
- What makes it unique

### 3. Define Your Audience

**Bad:**
```
Target Audience: Everyone
```

**Good:**
```
Target Audience: Coffee enthusiasts aged 25-45,
                 urban professionals who value
                 quality and sustainability
```

### 4. Choose the Right Tone

Match your brand and audience:
- **Professional**: B2B, luxury products, services
- **Casual**: Consumer products, apps, lifestyle
- **Playful**: Youth products, entertainment, games
- **Urgent**: Limited-time offers, flash sales
- **Inspirational**: Fitness, self-improvement, education

### 5. Use Brand Profiles

- Consistent results
- Better context for AI
- Saves time
- Maintains brand voice

### 6. Generate Multiple Variations

- Get 3-5 variations per campaign
- Pick the best or test multiple
- Different angles for different audiences

### 7. Review and Edit

- AI provides a strong starting point
- Customize to match your exact needs
- Add specific details or offers
- Adjust tone if needed

### 8. Test and Iterate

- Track which ads perform best
- Note what worked
- Use insights for next generation
- Refine your product descriptions

### 9. Platform-Specific Optimization

- Consider character limits
- Match platform tone
- Use appropriate CTAs
- Include platform-specific elements (hashtags, etc.)

### 10. Leverage Seasonal Context

Include timing in your description:
```
Product: Holiday gift box of premium chocolates,
         perfect for Christmas giving
```

## Troubleshooting

### I'm not receiving verification emails

**Solutions:**
1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Wait 5-10 minutes (delivery can be delayed)
3. Click "Resend Email" on verification page
4. Check email address is correct
5. Contact support if still not received

### Ad generation is taking too long

**Normal time:** 3-10 seconds

**If longer:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Refresh the page
3. Try again
4. Check if OpenAI is experiencing issues
5. Contact support if persistent

### Generated ads don't match my needs

**Try:**
1. Be more specific in product description
2. Add more details about target audience
3. Specify tone of voice
4. Use a brand profile for better context
5. Generate more variations
6. Edit the AI output to match your needs

### I can't generate more ads (out of credits)

**Options:**
1. Upgrade to a higher tier (Pricing page)
2. Purchase pay-per-ad credits ($1.99 each)
3. Wait for monthly renewal (if subscribed)
4. Contact support about your usage needs

### Payment failed

**Check:**
1. Card details are correct
2. Sufficient funds available
3. Card is not expired
4. Billing address matches card
5. Try a different payment method
6. Contact your bank
7. Contact support

### Can't access dashboard (logged out unexpectedly)

**Solutions:**
1. Log in again
2. Check if email is verified
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try incognito/private mode
5. Reset password if needed

### Subscription management portal not working

**Try:**
1. Ensure you have an active subscription
2. Use the button in Dashboard (not direct link)
3. Clear browser cookies
4. Try different browser
5. Contact support

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is AdCreatorPro really free to start?**
A: Yes! You get 3 free ad generations to try the platform. No credit card required.

**Q: What AI model does AdCreatorPro use?**
A: We use OpenAI's GPT-3.5 Turbo (Free-Pro tiers) and GPT-4 (Pro+ tiers) for the highest quality ad copy.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?**
A: Yes, you can cancel anytime from the subscription management portal. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

**Q: Do my ads expire?**
A: No, all your generated ads are saved in your history permanently. You can access them anytime.

**Q: Can I edit the AI-generated ads?**
A: Yes! The AI provides a starting point. Copy the text and edit it to perfectly match your needs.

### Features

**Q: How many variations should I generate?**
A: We recommend 3-5 variations for important campaigns. This gives you options for A/B testing.

**Q: What platforms are supported?**
A: Facebook/Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Ads, Twitter, TikTok, and Pinterest. Each generates platform-appropriate ad formats.

**Q: Can I use the same ad on multiple platforms?**
A: Yes, but we recommend generating platform-specific ads for best results. Each platform has different formats and audiences.

**Q: What's the difference between brand profiles and ad descriptions?**
A: Brand profiles save your company information for reuse. Product descriptions are specific to each ad you're generating.

### Billing

**Q: What happens when I run out of ads?**
A: For Free tier: You'll need to upgrade or purchase more ads. For paid tiers: Your count resets on your monthly billing date.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. Contact support with your request.

**Q: Do unused ads roll over to next month?**
A: No, your ad count resets on your billing date (for Starter tier). Pro/Business have unlimited ads.

**Q: Can I downgrade my plan?**
A: Yes, downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

### Technical

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: We support the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and secure authentication via Firebase. We never share your data.

**Q: Can I integrate AdCreatorPro with other tools?**
A: API access is coming soon for Business tier customers.

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
A: Your data is securely stored in Google Cloud Platform servers.
